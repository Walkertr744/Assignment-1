Community Spotlight: Neighborhood Cookout

Event Overview

Our annual neighborhood cookout brings together family, friends, and neighbors for an afternoon of good food, community and conversation. This year will be an old-money theme, so wear your Sunday Best and be prepared to enjoy an elegant afternoon. Our menu will feature: Grilled Steak Bites, Garlic Mashed Potatoes, and Seasoned Aspargus.

Featured Activities

  1. Croquet
  2. Live Jazz Music
  3. Estate-Style Photo Area
  4. Fireworks
  5. Wine Tasting

Attendee Tips

  • Dress to impress -- Wear classic, elegant clothing such as polos, linen shirts, sundresses etc...
  • Bring your best lawn game skills -- Come ready to play croquet, because the winner will receive a $50 giftcard to Amazon
  • Bring a blanket or lawn chair -- Once the sun sets we will light up to sky with fireworks!
  • Enjoy the community -- Arrive ready to mingle, take time to meet your neighbors, and unplug for a few hours
